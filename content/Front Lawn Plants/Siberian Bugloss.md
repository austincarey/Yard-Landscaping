# Siberian Bugloss

**Scientific Name:** *Brunnera macrophylla*
**Category:** Herbaceous Perennial
![[Pasted image 20260426151903.png]]

## Overview
A non-native perennial highly valued for its tolerance of pine tree root competition and dry shade. It features large, heart-shaped leaves and delicate, bright blue flowers in early spring that resemble forget-me-nots.

## Details
- **Light Requirements:** Part Shade to Full Shade
- **Water Needs:** Moderate
- **Soil Tolerance:** Adaptable, handles clay
- **Height:** 1 - 1.5 feet
- **Spread:** 1.5 - 2 feet
- **Bloom Time:** April - May (bright blue flowers)

## Care Guide
- **Planting:** Can handle the difficult, dry spots under pines once established. The large leaves provide a great textural contrast to pine needles.
- **Watering:** Will need supplemental water during extreme dry periods or heat waves.
- **Maintenance:** Remove old foliage in late winter or early spring before new growth and flowers emerge.
- **Ecological Value:** Early blooms offer nectar for emerging bees.
