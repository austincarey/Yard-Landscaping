# Wild Bergamot

**Scientific Name:** *Monarda fistulosa*
**Category:** Herbaceous Perennial
![[Pasted image 20260426150724.png]]

## Overview
A reliable performer in the unpredictable Colorado climate. It possesses highly aromatic, oregano-scented foliage that is naturally deer and rabbit resistant, with shaggy lavender flowers.

## Details
- **Light Requirements:** Part Sun to Full Sun
- **Water Needs:** Low to Moderate
- **Soil Tolerance:** Highly adaptable, including heavy clay
- **Height:** 2 - 3 feet
- **Spread:** 2 - 3 feet (spreads via rhizomes)
- **Bloom Time:** July - August

## Care Guide
- **Planting:** Needs room to spread. Good for transition zones.
- **Watering:** Tolerates a wide range of moisture conditions, from dry to moist.
- **Maintenance:** Can be prone to powdery mildew if airflow is poor. Divide clumps every few years to maintain vigor.
- **Ecological Value:** A magnet for bees, butterflies, and hummingbirds.
