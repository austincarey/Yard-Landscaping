# Hummingbird Trumpet

**Scientific Name:** *Epilobium canum* (formerly *Zauschneria*)
**Category:** Herbaceous Perennial
![[Pasted image 20260426151821.png]]

## Overview
While it typically prefers sun, this tough native is highly adaptable and can handle dry shade well. It’s a fantastic late-season bloomer, offering bright orange or red tubular flowers when most other plants have finished blooming.

## Details
- **Light Requirements:** Full Sun to Part Shade (tolerates dry shade)
- **Water Needs:** Low / Xeric
- **Soil Tolerance:** Well-drained, adaptable to clay if not overly wet
- **Height:** 1 - 2 feet
- **Spread:** 1 - 2 feet
- **Bloom Time:** August - October (red/orange flowers)

## Care Guide
- **Planting:** Performs best in lean, well-draining soils but can handle clay pockets if situated on a slight slope or mound.
- **Watering:** Extremely drought-tolerant once established.
- **Maintenance:** Cut back to the ground in late winter or early spring before new growth begins.
- **Ecological Value:** A critical late-season nectar source for migrating hummingbirds.
