# Serviceberry

**Scientific Name:** *Amelanchier alnifolia* (Saskatoon Serviceberry)
**Category:** Shrub
![[Pasted image 20260426150525.png]]

## Overview
One of the most versatile native shrubs for the Front Range. Naturally found in the understory of coniferous forests, it possesses a high degree of shade tolerance and provides multiple seasons of interest.

## Details
- **Light Requirements:** Part Shade to Full Sun
- **Water Needs:** Low to Moderate
- **Soil Tolerance:** Adaptable, tolerates alkaline soils
- **Height:** 6 - 10 feet
- **Spread:** 5 - 8 feet
- **Bloom Time:** April (white flower clusters)

## Care Guide
- **Planting:** Use as a structural "backbone" plant or small understory tree at the edges of the pine canopy.
- **Watering:** Provide moderate moisture during the first few years; drought-tolerant once mature.
- **Maintenance:** Prune for shape in late winter or early spring if desired.
- **Ecological Value:** Blue-black fruit is edible for humans (tasting similar to blueberries) and highly attractive to wildlife. Brilliant orange/red fall foliage.
