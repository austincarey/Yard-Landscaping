# Firecracker Penstemon

**Scientific Name:** *Penstemon eatonii*
**Category:** Additional Pollinator Plant
![[Pasted image 20260426145926.png]]

## Overview
A spectacular native perennial that produces tall spikes of bright red, tubular flowers. It is incredibly tough and highly drought-resistant, perfect for xeric gardens.

## Details
- **Light Requirements:** Full Sun
- **Water Needs:** Low / Xeric
- **Soil Tolerance:** Requires excellent drainage; prefers lean, sandy, or rocky soils
- **Height:** 2 - 3 feet (in bloom)
- **Spread:** 1 - 2 feet
- **Bloom Time:** Late Spring to Early Summer

## Care Guide
- **Planting:** Must have good drainage to survive. Do not plant in heavy, wet clay or low spots.
- **Watering:** Needs very little water. Do not overwater.
- **Maintenance:** Cut back flower stalks after they finish blooming if you don't want seeds to spread, or leave them for winter interest.
- **Ecological Value:** A primary energy source for Broad-tailed and Rufous hummingbirds during their residency and migration.
