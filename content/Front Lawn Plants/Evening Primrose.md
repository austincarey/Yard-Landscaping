# Evening Primrose

**Scientific Name:** *Oenothera* species
**Category:** Additional Pollinator Plant
![[Pasted image 20260426145843.png]]

## Overview
A resilient native plant featuring large, delicate, usually white or yellow flowers that often open in the late afternoon or evening. Excellent for hot, dry, and poor soils.

## Details
- **Light Requirements:** Full Sun
- **Water Needs:** Low / Xeric
- **Soil Tolerance:** Very adaptable to poor, sandy, or rocky soils
- **Height:** 1 - 2 feet
- **Spread:** 1 - 2 feet
- **Bloom Time:** Summer

## Care Guide
- **Planting:** Great for the sunniest and driest transition zones.
- **Watering:** Very drought-tolerant. Overwatering will kill it.
- **Maintenance:** Can be aggressive spreaders or re-seeders in the right conditions.
- **Ecological Value:** Flowers are primarily pollinated by Sphinx Moths and other nocturnal or crepuscular insects.
