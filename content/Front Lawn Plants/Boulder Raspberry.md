# Boulder Raspberry

**Scientific Name:** *Rubus deliciosus*
**Category:** Shrub
![[Pasted image 20260426145529.png]]
## Overview
Native to the rocky outcrops of the Front Range foothills, the Boulder Raspberry is a spineless, arching shrub uniquely adapted to life under large trees. It features large, showy, white rose-like flowers and distinctive peeling bark.

## Details
- **Light Requirements:** Part Shade to Full Shade
- **Water Needs:** Low / Xeric
- **Soil Tolerance:** Thrives in rocky, well-drained soils
- **Height:** 3 - 6 feet
- **Spread:** 3 - 6 feet
- **Bloom Time:** Late Spring

## Care Guide
- **Planting:** Exceptionally shade-tolerant. Can thrive in the "dry shade" pockets where other shrubs might become spindly or weak.
- **Watering:** Very drought tolerant. Water during long dry spells.
- **Maintenance:** Prune out older, woody canes to encourage new growth and better flowering.
- **Ecological Value:** Large flowers attract bees and butterflies. Cinnamon-colored, peeling bark provides significant winter interest.
