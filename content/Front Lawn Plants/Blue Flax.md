# Blue Flax

**Scientific Name:** *Linum lewisii*
**Category:** Herbaceous Perennial
![[Pasted image 20260426145203.png]]
## Overview
A delicate-looking but tough native perennial that produces a succession of beautiful sky-blue flowers. Each flower lasts only a day, but new ones open continually throughout the bloom season.

## Details
- **Light Requirements:** Full Sun to Part Sun
- **Water Needs:** Low / Xeric
- **Soil Tolerance:** Prefers well-draining, sandy or rocky soils
- **Height:** 1 - 2 feet
- **Spread:** 1 foot
- **Bloom Time:** Late Spring to Mid-Summer

## Care Guide
- **Planting:** Very easy to establish from seed sown directly onto prepared soil.
- **Watering:** Drought-tolerant. Needs very little water once established.
- **Maintenance:** Re-seeds itself readily if conditions are right.
- **Ecological Value:** Attracts bees and butterflies. Often an early succession plant in disturbed areas.
