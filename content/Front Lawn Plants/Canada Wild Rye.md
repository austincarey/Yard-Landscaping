# Canada Wild Rye

**Scientific Name:** *Elymus canadensis*
**Category:** Grass
![[Pasted image 20260426145610.png]]

## Overview
A hardy native grass commonly found in the pine and Douglas fir forests of the mountains. It produces elegant, drooping seed heads that resemble wheat, adding movement and texture to a shade garden.

## Details
- **Light Requirements:** Full Sun to Part Shade
- **Water Needs:** Moderate
- **Soil Tolerance:** Adaptable to a wide variety of soils
- **Height:** 3 - 4 feet
- **Spread:** 2 - 3 feet
- **Bloom Time:** Mid to Late Summer (seed heads)

## Care Guide
- **Planting:** Easy to establish from seed. Provides important structure and height to a shade garden.
- **Watering:** Prefers moderate moisture but has good drought tolerance once established.
- **Maintenance:** Cut back to the ground in late winter or early spring before new growth begins.
- **Ecological Value:** Provides cover for wildlife. The seeds are eaten by birds and small mammals.
