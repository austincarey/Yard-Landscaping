# Blanket Flower

**Scientific Name:** *Gaillardia aristata*
**Category:** Herbaceous Perennial
![[Pasted image 20260426145120.png]]

## Overview
A rugged, sun-loving perennial featuring daisy-like flowers with vibrant red centers and yellow tips. It handles higher light intensity and the clay-heavy soils of the transition zones perfectly.

## Details
- **Light Requirements:** Full Sun
- **Water Needs:** Low / Xeric
- **Soil Tolerance:** Adaptable, thrives in poor or clay soils as long as they are relatively well-drained
- **Height:** 1 - 2 feet
- **Spread:** 1 - 2 feet
- **Bloom Time:** June - September

## Care Guide
- **Planting:** Plant in sun-exposed transition zones away from the deepest shade of the pines.
- **Watering:** Highly drought-tolerant. Water deeply but infrequently once established.
- **Maintenance:** Deadheading can prolong the bloom period, but leaving seeds provides winter interest and food for birds.
- **Ecological Value:** The specific host plant for the Gaillardia Moth. Attracts a wide variety of native bees and butterflies.
