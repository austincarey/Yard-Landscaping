# Plains Oval Sedge

**Scientific Name:** *Carex brevior*
**Category:** Sedge
![[Pasted image 20260426150220.png]]

## Overview
A robust sedge that can handle a wide range of light conditions, from full sun to nearly full shade. It is a clump-forming species that can serve as a native alternative to turfgrass.

## Details
- **Light Requirements:** Full Sun to Part Shade
- **Water Needs:** Low to Moderate
- **Soil Tolerance:** Adaptable to various soil types
- **Height:** 1 - 2 feet
- **Spread:** 1 - 2 feet
- **Bloom Time:** Late Spring

## Care Guide
- **Planting:** Ideal for areas that do not receive heavy foot traffic.
- **Watering:** Water to establish, then drought-tolerant.
- **Maintenance:** Can be left un-mowed for a natural meadow look, or mowed high a few times a year.
- **Ecological Value:** Provides habitat and structure in the lower plant layer. Seeds provide food for wildlife.
