# Chokecherry

**Scientific Name:** *Prunus virginiana*
**Category:** Shrub / Small Tree
![[Pasted image 20260426151550.png]]

## Overview
A larger native shrub that can adapt well to the understory of pine trees. It features beautiful, elongated clusters of white flowers in the spring followed by dark, tart cherries. 

## Details
- **Light Requirements:** Full Sun to Part Shade (tolerates full shade)
- **Water Needs:** Moderate to Low
- **Soil Tolerance:** Adaptable, clay tolerant
- **Height:** 15 - 25 feet
- **Spread:** 10 - 15 feet
- **Bloom Time:** May - June (white flowers)

## Care Guide
- **Planting:** Ensure it has enough space, as it can spread via underground runners and form thickets.
- **Watering:** Moderate water needs, though established thickets are quite drought tolerant.
- **Maintenance:** May need root pruning if spreading is not desired. Prune out dead wood to keep it vigorous.
- **Ecological Value:** High value for wildlife. Flowers feed early pollinators; berries are heavily consumed by birds.
