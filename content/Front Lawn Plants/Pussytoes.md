# Pussytoes

**Scientific Name:** *Antennaria* species (e.g., *Antennaria parvifolia*, *Antennaria rosea*)
**Category:** Ground Cover
![[Pasted image 20260426150343.png]]

## Overview
Pussytoes are low-growing, spreading perennials that form tight, silvery-gray mats of foliage. They are invaluable for the sun-exposed and transition areas of the lawn, reflecting intense sunlight and retaining moisture.

## Details
- **Light Requirements:** Full Sun to Part Sun
- **Water Needs:** Extremely Low / Xeric
- **Soil Tolerance:** Thrives in lean, poor, compacted soils
- **Height:** 1 - 3 inches (foliage); up to 6-12 inches (flower stalks)
- **Spread:** 1 - 2 feet (spreads via runners)
- **Bloom Time:** Late Spring

## Care Guide
- **Planting:** Ideal for replacing grass in compacted or challenging transition zones without deep soil disturbance.
- **Watering:** Highly drought-tolerant. Only requires water during establishment or extreme extended droughts.
- **Maintenance:** Fast-spreading but easy to manage. Requires minimal care.
- **Ecological Value:** Flowers provide nectar for small butterflies and bees. Acts as a "living mulch" to cool soil and retain moisture for nearby plants.
