# Wax Currant

**Scientific Name:** *Ribes cereum*
**Category:** Shrub
![[Pasted image 20260426150656.png]]

## Overview
A compact, mounded native shrub with small, lobed, leathery leaves and pink tubular flowers. It is excellent for dry, rocky areas.

## Details
- **Light Requirements:** Full Sun to Part Shade
- **Water Needs:** Low / Xeric
- **Soil Tolerance:** Adaptable to dry, rocky, and alkaline soils
- **Height:** 2 - 4 feet
- **Spread:** 2 - 4 feet
- **Bloom Time:** Early Spring (pink tubular flowers)

## Care Guide
- **Planting:** Great as a structural element in transition zones or near the edges of a pine canopy.
- **Watering:** Very drought-tolerant once established.
- **Maintenance:** Minimal pruning required.
- **Ecological Value:** Flowers are a food source for hummingbirds and native bees. Produces fruit that is a staple for local birds. Offers excellent fall color.
