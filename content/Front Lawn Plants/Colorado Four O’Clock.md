# Colorado Four O’Clock

**Scientific Name:** *Mirabilis multiflora*
**Category:** Additional Pollinator Plant
![[Pasted image 20260426145707.png]]

## Overview
A large, sprawling, native perennial known for its magenta, funnel-shaped flowers that open in the late afternoon. It is an extremely deep-rooted and drought-tolerant plant.

## Details
- **Light Requirements:** Full Sun to Part Shade
- **Water Needs:** Extremely Low / Xeric
- **Soil Tolerance:** Thrives in dry, rocky, well-draining soils
- **Height:** 1.5 - 3 feet
- **Spread:** 3 - 6 feet
- **Bloom Time:** Mid-Summer to Early Fall

## Care Guide
- **Planting:** Give it plenty of room to spread. Its large taproot makes it difficult to transplant once established.
- **Watering:** Requires almost no supplemental water once established. Do not overwater.
- **Maintenance:** Dies back entirely to the ground in winter. Emerges late in the spring.
- **Ecological Value:** Specifically attractive to Sphinx Moths, which are important nocturnal pollinators.
