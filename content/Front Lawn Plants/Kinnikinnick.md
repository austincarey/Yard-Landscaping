# Kinnikinnick

**Scientific Name:** *Arctostaphylos uva-ursi*
**Category:** Ground Cover
![[Pasted image 20260426150136.png]]

## Overview
Kinnikinnick is an exemplary evergreen ground cover native to the Colorado foothills and montane zones. It forms dense, low mats of leathery, waxy leaves that are highly resistant to desiccation.

## Details
- **Light Requirements:** Part Shade to Dry Shade (excellent under pines)
- **Water Needs:** Low / Xeric once established
- **Soil Tolerance:** Well-drained, sandy, gravelly, or slightly acidic soils
- **Height:** 4 - 6 inches
- **Spread:** 2 - 4 feet (or more over time)
- **Bloom Time:** Early Spring (white-to-pink urn-shaped flowers)

## Care Guide
- **Planting:** Slow to establish. Best planted from small plugs or small pots rather than large containers.
- **Watering:** Provide regular moisture during the first year to establish a deep root system. Once established, requires virtually no supplemental watering.
- **Maintenance:** Extremely low maintenance. Avoid walking on it frequently.
- **Ecological Value:** Early spring flowers provide essential nectar for early-emerging bees. Bright red berries in late summer persist into winter, offering forage for local birds and mammals.
