# Blue Mist Penstemon

**Scientific Name:** *Penstemon virens*
**Category:** Herbaceous Perennial
![[Pasted image 20260426145238.png]]

## Overview
Specifically adapted to the understory of Ponderosa pines in the foothills, Blue Mist Penstemon is a superior option for drier, deeper shade. It forms small clumps of dark green foliage with vibrant flowers.

## Details
- **Light Requirements:** Part Shade to Dry Shade
- **Water Needs:** Low / Xeric
- **Soil Tolerance:** Well-drained, poor, rocky soils
- **Height:** 6 - 12 inches
- **Spread:** 6 - 12 inches
- **Bloom Time:** May - June (sky-blue flowers)

## Care Guide
- **Planting:** Does not tolerate poorly drained or heavy, wet clay soils. Plant in amended, well-draining spots.
- **Watering:** Very drought-tolerant once established. Avoid overwatering.
- **Maintenance:** Requires very little maintenance. Cut back spent flower stalks if desired.
- **Ecological Value:** Excellent nectar source for native bees and hummingbirds.
