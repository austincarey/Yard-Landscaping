# Wild Geranium

**Scientific Name:** *Geranium caespitosum*
**Category:** Herbaceous Perennial
![[Pasted image 20260426150759.png]]

## Overview
A forest-dwelling native that excels in dry shade. It features deeply lobed foliage that greens up early in the spring, followed by pink-to-lavender flowers that bloom throughout the summer.

## Details
- **Light Requirements:** Part Shade to Full Shade
- **Water Needs:** Low to Moderate
- **Soil Tolerance:** Adaptable to various soils, including rocky or uneven areas
- **Height:** 1 - 2 feet
- **Spread:** 1 - 2 feet
- **Bloom Time:** June - August

## Care Guide
- **Planting:** Excellent for filling in rocky or uneven areas beneath a tree canopy over time.
- **Watering:** Provide moderate water during establishment; tolerates drought well once mature.
- **Maintenance:** Known for its ability to re-seed itself gently. Leave some seed heads if spreading is desired.
- **Ecological Value:** Attracts native bees and butterflies over a long blooming season.
