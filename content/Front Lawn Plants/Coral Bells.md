# Coral Bells

**Scientific Name:** *Heuchera* species/cultivars
**Category:** Herbaceous Perennial
![[Pasted image 20260426151631.png]]

## Overview
While many available varieties are cultivars, they are absolute champions for dry shade under pines. They send up delicate spikes of tiny, bell-shaped flowers, but are primarily grown for their vibrant foliage that persists well into winter.

## Details
- **Light Requirements:** Part Shade to Full Shade
- **Water Needs:** Low to Moderate
- **Soil Tolerance:** Adaptable, needs good drainage
- **Height:** 1 - 2 feet
- **Spread:** 1 - 2 feet
- **Bloom Time:** June - July (white, pink, or red flowers)

## Care Guide
- **Planting:** Plant shallowly with the crown slightly above the soil line to prevent rot.
- **Watering:** Needs regular water to establish, but mature plants handle dry shade effectively.
- **Maintenance:** Remove old, tattered leaves in the spring. Divide every 3-4 years if the crown becomes woody or lifts out of the ground.
- **Ecological Value:** Provides some nectar for bees; dense foliage offers cover for beneficial insects.
