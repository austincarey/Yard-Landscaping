# Rocky Mountain Columbine

**Scientific Name:** *Aquilegia caerulea*
**Category:** Herbaceous Perennial
![[Pasted image 20260426150454.png]]

## Overview
The state flower of Colorado, Rocky Mountain Columbine is culturally significant and ecologically vital. It features elegant, spurred blue and white flowers and thrives in dappled shade.

## Details
- **Light Requirements:** Dappled Shade to Part Shade
- **Water Needs:** Moderate ("moist shade")
- **Soil Tolerance:** Prefers rich, loamy, well-draining soil
- **Height:** 1 - 2 feet
- **Spread:** 1 - 1.5 feet
- **Bloom Time:** Late Spring to Early Summer

## Care Guide
- **Planting:** Best placed near a downspout or given supplemental irrigation through a drip system when planted under pines.
- **Watering:** Requires consistent moisture, especially during the hot summer months.
- **Maintenance:** Deadhead spent flowers to encourage further blooming, or leave seed pods to allow for self-seeding.
- **Ecological Value:** A primary nectar source for hummingbirds and long-tongued bees.
