# Common Juniper

**Scientific Name:** *Juniperus communis*
**Category:** Shrub
![[Pasted image 20260426145734.png]]

## Overview
A low-growing, evergreen native shrub that provides structure, ground cover, and year-round interest. It is incredibly tough and adaptable to challenging conditions.

## Details
- **Light Requirements:** Full Sun to Part Shade
- **Water Needs:** Extremely Low / Xeric
- **Soil Tolerance:** Highly adaptable to poor, rocky, and dry soils
- **Height:** 1 - 3 feet
- **Spread:** 4 - 8 feet
- **Bloom Time:** Non-flowering (produces berry-like cones)

## Care Guide
- **Planting:** Excellent for stabilizing slopes or filling large, dry areas. Give it plenty of room to spread.
- **Watering:** Needs water only during the establishment phase.
- **Maintenance:** Virtually maintenance-free.
- **Ecological Value:** Often overlooked, native junipers are host plants for a significant number of caterpillar species in Colorado. Birds also eat the berry-like cones.
