# Smooth Aster

**Scientific Name:** *Symphyotrichum laeve*
**Category:** Herbaceous Perennial
![[Pasted image 20260426150548.png]]

## Overview
Essential for late-season color, the Smooth Aster produces a profusion of blue-purple, daisy-like flowers from August through October. It is aggressive and hardy, outcompeting weeds in transition zones.

## Details
- **Light Requirements:** Part Sun to Full Sun
- **Water Needs:** Low to Moderate
- **Soil Tolerance:** Adaptable to poor, dry, or clay soils
- **Height:** 2 - 3 feet
- **Spread:** 1 - 2 feet
- **Bloom Time:** Late Summer to Fall

## Care Guide
- **Planting:** Great for the sunnier edges of the pine canopy.
- **Watering:** Water regularly during establishment. Tolerates drought but blooms best with occasional watering during dry spells.
- **Maintenance:** Can be pinched back in early summer to encourage a bushier habit and more blooms.
- **Ecological Value:** Provides a critical late-season nectar source for pollinators preparing for winter or migration.
