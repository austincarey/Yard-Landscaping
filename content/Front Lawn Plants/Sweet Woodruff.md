# Sweet Woodruff

**Scientific Name:** *Galium odoratum*
**Category:** Herbaceous Perennial / Groundcover
![[Pasted image 20260426152016.png]]

## Overview
A classic dry-shade groundcover. It forms a dense, low mat of bright green, whorled leaves that are slightly fragrant when crushed. In the spring, it is blanketed in tiny, star-shaped white flowers.

## Details
- **Light Requirements:** Part Shade to Full Shade
- **Water Needs:** Moderate
- **Soil Tolerance:** Adaptable to various soil types
- **Height:** 0.5 - 1 foot
- **Spread:** 1 - 2 feet (spreads steadily)
- **Bloom Time:** April - May (white flowers)

## Care Guide
- **Planting:** Great for forming a living mulch layer under pine trees to suppress weeds.
- **Watering:** Needs regular moisture to get established, then becomes quite tolerant of dry shade.
- **Maintenance:** Can be aggressive if conditions are perfect, but easily managed by pulling out the shallow roots where it is unwanted.
- **Ecological Value:** Forms dense mats providing habitat for ground-dwelling beneficial insects.
