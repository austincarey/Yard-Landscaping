# Big-root Geranium

**Scientific Name:** *Geranium macrorrhizum*
**Category:** Herbaceous Perennial
![[Pasted image 20260426151517.png]]

## Overview
A non-native relative to the native Wild Geranium, this species is renowned in landscaping for being virtually indestructible in dry shade. It thrives despite heavy root competition from trees, offering clusters of pink or magenta flowers and strongly scented foliage.

## Details
- **Light Requirements:** Part Shade to Full Shade
- **Water Needs:** Low
- **Soil Tolerance:** Highly adaptable, easily handles clay and root competition
- **Height:** 1 - 1.5 feet
- **Spread:** 1.5 - 2 feet
- **Bloom Time:** May - June (pink/magenta flowers)

## Care Guide
- **Planting:** An excellent problem-solver for the toughest, driest spots directly under pine canopies.
- **Watering:** Very drought-tolerant once its fleshy rhizomes are established.
- **Maintenance:** Virtually maintenance-free. Spreads to form a dense weed-suppressing mat.
- **Ecological Value:** Aromatic foliage is highly deer and rabbit resistant.
