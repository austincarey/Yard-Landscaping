# Harebells

**Scientific Name:** *Campanula rotundifolia*
**Category:** Herbaceous Perennial
![[Pasted image 20260426151718.png]]

## Overview
A delicate-looking but incredibly tough native perennial that produces lovely, nodding blue bell-shaped flowers. Despite its fragile appearance, it thrives in dry, shady spots, including under mature pines.

## Details
- **Light Requirements:** Full Sun to Part Shade (handles dry shade well)
- **Water Needs:** Low
- **Soil Tolerance:** Adaptable, handles rocky and clay soils
- **Height:** 1 - 2 feet
- **Spread:** 1 foot
- **Bloom Time:** June - September (blue flowers)

## Care Guide
- **Planting:** Can be planted in pockets between major tree roots.
- **Watering:** Very drought tolerant once established, but occasional deep watering during extreme drought will prolong the bloom.
- **Maintenance:** Deadheading can encourage a longer bloom time, though it's not strictly necessary.
- **Ecological Value:** Attracts native bees and hummingbirds.
