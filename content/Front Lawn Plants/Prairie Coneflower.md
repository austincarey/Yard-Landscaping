# Prairie Coneflower

**Scientific Name:** *Ratibida columnifera*
**Category:** Herbaceous Perennial
![[Pasted image 20260426150318.png]]

## Overview
A distinctive, drought-tolerant perennial featuring a prominent, elongated central cone surrounded by drooping yellow or red-brown petals. Very hardy for the Western High Plains.

## Details
- **Light Requirements:** Full Sun
- **Water Needs:** Low / Xeric
- **Soil Tolerance:** Excellent in dry, poor, or sandy soils
- **Height:** 1 - 2 feet
- **Spread:** 1 - 1.5 feet
- **Bloom Time:** Summer to Early Fall

## Care Guide
- **Planting:** Use in the hottest, sunniest transition zones of the lawn.
- **Watering:** Highly drought-resistant once established.
- **Maintenance:** Very low maintenance. Leave seed heads for winter interest and bird forage.
- **Ecological Value:** Attracts native bees and butterflies; seeds are eaten by finches and other small birds.
