# Ivory Sedge

**Scientific Name:** *Carex eburnea*
**Category:** Sedge
![[Pasted image 20260426150050.png]]
## Overview
A diminutive, fine-textured sedge that forms soft, hair-like mounds. It is one of the most shade-tolerant native species available and remarkably drought-resistant once established.

## Details
- **Light Requirements:** Part Shade to Full Shade
- **Water Needs:** Low to Moderate
- **Soil Tolerance:** Prefers well-drained soils; tolerates alkaline conditions
- **Height:** 4 - 8 inches
- **Spread:** 6 - 12 inches
- **Bloom Time:** Early Spring (inconspicuous)

## Care Guide
- **Planting:** Works well as an accent in a rock garden or as a soft border along a path in deep shade.
- **Watering:** Moderate watering during establishment. Tolerates dry shade well.
- **Maintenance:** Does not require mowing. Can be cut back in late winter to tidy up.
- **Ecological Value:** Provides cover for small insects and a fine-textured aesthetic alternative to turfgrass under pine trees.
