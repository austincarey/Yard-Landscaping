# Golden Currant

**Scientific Name:** *Ribes aureum*
**Category:** Shrub
![[Pasted image 20260426145956.png]]

## Overview
A favorite among Colorado gardeners for its early spring flowers, which fill the air with a spicy, clove-like scent. An extremely drought-tolerant shrub that handles alkaline, clay soils with minimal amendment.

## Details
- **Light Requirements:** Full Sun to Part Shade
- **Water Needs:** Low / Xeric
- **Soil Tolerance:** Adaptable to heavy clay and alkaline conditions
- **Height:** 4 - 6 feet
- **Spread:** 4 - 6 feet
- **Bloom Time:** Early Spring

## Care Guide
- **Planting:** A good filler for the edges of a pine canopy.
- **Watering:** Very drought tolerant once established.
- **Maintenance:** Prune out older canes every few years to encourage vigorous new growth and better flowering.
- **Ecological Value:** Leaves provide food for the larvae of several butterfly species; flowers feed adult pollinators. Produces edible fruit that is a staple for local birds. Excellent fall color.
