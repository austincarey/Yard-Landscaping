# Snowberry

**Scientific Name:** *Symphoricarpos albus*
**Category:** Shrub
![[Pasted image 20260426151944.png]]

## Overview
A remarkably tough, native drought-tolerant shrub that handles shade and clay effortlessly. It produces small, bell-shaped pinkish-white flowers in the summer, followed by striking white berries that persist into winter.

## Details
- **Light Requirements:** Full Sun to Full Shade
- **Water Needs:** Low to Moderate
- **Soil Tolerance:** Highly adaptable, handles clay well
- **Height:** 3 - 5 feet
- **Spread:** 3 - 5 feet
- **Bloom Time:** June - August (pinkish-white flowers)

## Care Guide
- **Planting:** Very easy to establish. Can handle root competition under pines.
- **Watering:** Provide regular water during the first year, after which it is very drought-tolerant.
- **Maintenance:** Prune in late winter or early spring if needed to control shape or spread.
- **Ecological Value:** Flowers attract pollinators; berries are an important winter food source for birds.
