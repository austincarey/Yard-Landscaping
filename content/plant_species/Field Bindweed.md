# Field Bindweed

**Scientific Name:** *Convolvulus arvensis*
**Type:** Weed (Highly Invasive)
**Native to Colorado:** No

## Weed Extermination
Deep, extensive root system makes hand pulling ineffective. Requires persistent use of systemic herbicides (glyphosate, quinclorac) or heavy, multi-year mulching/solarization.
