# SteppeSuns Sunset Glow Penstemon

**Scientific Name:** *Penstemon pinifolius 'P019S'*
**Type:** Perennial
**Native to Colorado:** Plant Select

**Flower Color:** Orange
**Bloom Time:** Summer
**Height:** 12 - 18 inches
**Width:** 18 - 24 inches

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun.
- **Soil:** Well-drained, non-clay soil preferred.
- **Water:** Drought-tolerant (xeric).
- **Planting Guide:** Fine, evergreen foliage.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Hand-trim brown/dead stems in spring.
- **Other Info:** Pollinator-friendly.
