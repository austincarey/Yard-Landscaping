# Garden Tulip

**Scientific Name:** *Tulipa gesneriana*
**Type:** Perennial Bulb
**Native to Colorado:** No

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun to partial shade.
- **Soil:** Rich, well-drained soil.
- **Water:** Moderate in spring, but must stay dry during summer dormancy.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Remove spent flowers, but leave foliage until it turns yellow and dies back naturally to feed the bulb.
- **Fertilizing:** Apply bulb food in fall when planting, and a light application in early spring as shoots emerge.
