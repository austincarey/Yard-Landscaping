# Dog Rose

**Scientific Name:** *Rosa canina*
**Type:** Shrub
**Native to Colorado:** No

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun.
- **Soil:** Adaptable, but prefers well-drained loam.
- **Water:** Moderate. Very hardy once established.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Prune in late winter or early spring to remove dead wood and shape. Produces edible rose hips in fall.
- **Fertilizing:** Apply rose fertilizer in spring.
