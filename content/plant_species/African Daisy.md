# African Daisy

**Scientific Name:** *Dimorphotheca sinuata*
**Type:** Annual
**Seed Mix:** [[Wildflower Seeds|Colorado Wildflower Mix]]
**Native to Colorado:** No

**Flower Color:** Orange, Yellow, Apricot
**Bloom Time:** Spring to Mid-Summer

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun. (Blooms may close on cloudy days or in late afternoon).
- **Soil:** Very well-drained soil; poor soils are tolerated well.
- **Water:** Low. Highly drought tolerant.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Deadhead to extend the blooming season. Great for hot, dry spots in the yard.
