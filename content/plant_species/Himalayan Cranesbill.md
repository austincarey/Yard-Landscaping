# Himalayan Cranesbill

**Scientific Name:** *Geranium himalayense*
**Type:** Perennial
**Native to Colorado:** No

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun to partial shade.
- **Soil:** Organically rich, well-drained soil.
- **Water:** Moderate. Needs regular watering, especially during hot, dry spells.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Cut back foliage significantly in mid-summer if it looks scraggly to encourage fresh new growth.
- **Fertilizing:** Apply balanced fertilizer in early spring as new growth emerges.
