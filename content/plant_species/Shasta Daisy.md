# Shasta Daisy

**Scientific Name:** *Chrysanthemum maximum* (or *Leucanthemum x superbum*)
**Type:** Perennial
**Seed Mix:** [[Wildflower Seeds|Colorado Wildflower Mix]]
**Native to Colorado:** No

**Flower Color:** White with yellow center
**Bloom Time:** Early to Late Summer

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun.
- **Soil:** Moderately fertile, well-drained soil.
- **Water:** Moderate. Needs more consistent moisture than some native wildflowers.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Deadhead regularly to extend bloom period. Divide every 2-3 years to maintain vigor.
