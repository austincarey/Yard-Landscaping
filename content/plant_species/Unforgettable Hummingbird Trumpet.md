# Unforgettable Hummingbird Trumpet

**Scientific Name:** *Epilobium canum 'Unforgettable'*
**Type:** Perennial
**Native to Colorado:** Plant Select

**Flower Color:** Scarlet-orange
**Bloom Time:** Late Summer to Frost
**Height:** 24 inches
**Width:** 18 inches

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun (loves heat).
- **Soil:** Well-drained.
- **Water:** Drought-tolerant once established.
- **Planting Guide:** Placement near hardscape encourages blooms.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Vigorous, upright form.
- **Other Info:** Very cold hardy. Critical late-season nectar for hummingbirds. Deer and rabbit resistant.
