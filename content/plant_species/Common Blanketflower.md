# Common Blanketflower

**Scientific Name:** *Gaillardia aristata*
**Type:** Perennial
**Seed Mix:** [[Wildflower Seeds|Colorado Wildflower Mix]]
**Native to Colorado:** Yes

**Flower Color:** Red with Yellow tips
**Bloom Time:** Early Summer to Fall

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun.
- **Soil:** Well-drained soils, excellent in rocky/poor soils.
- **Water:** Low. Very drought tolerant.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Deadhead to prolong blooming season.
- **Fertilizing:** None needed.
