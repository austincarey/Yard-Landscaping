# Yarrow

**Scientific Name:** *Achillea millefolium*
**Type:** Perennial
**Native to Colorado:** Yes

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun.
- **Soil:** Dry, well-drained soils. Intolerant of wet, heavy clay.
- **Water:** Low. Excellent drought tolerance.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Deadhead to encourage continuous blooming. Divide clumps every 3-5 years.
- **Fertilizing:** Do not fertilize; rich soil causes floppy, weak stems.
