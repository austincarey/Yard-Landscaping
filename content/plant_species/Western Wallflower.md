# Western Wallflower

**Scientific Name:** *Erysimum asperum*
**Type:** Biennial/Perennial
**Native to Colorado:** Yes

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun.
- **Soil:** Dry, rocky, poor soils.
- **Water:** Low.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Pinch back early to encourage bushiness. Cut back after flowering.
- **Fertilizing:** None needed.
