# Wild Thing Sage

**Scientific Name:** *Salvia greggii 'Wild Thing'*
**Type:** Perennial
**Native to Colorado:** Plant Select

**Flower Color:** Hot pink to magenta
**Bloom Time:** Late Spring through October
**Height:** 18 - 24 inches
**Width:** 18 - 24 inches

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun to partial shade.
- **Soil:** Well-drained.
- **Water:** Low water once established. Highly drought-tolerant.
- **Planting Guide:** Ideal for xeriscaping.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Cut back by 1/3 to 1/2 in spring after weather warms.
- **Other Info:** Attracts hummingbirds and butterflies. Deer and rabbit resistant.
