# Scarlet Flax

**Scientific Name:** *Linum grandiflorum rubrum*
**Type:** Annual
**Seed Mix:** [[Wildflower Seeds|Colorado Wildflower Mix]]
**Native to Colorado:** No

**Flower Color:** Scarlet Red
**Bloom Time:** Spring to Mid-Summer

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun.
- **Soil:** Well-drained, sandy or loamy soils.
- **Water:** Low to moderate.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Short-lived blooms but flowers continuously. Will easily self-sow for the following year.
