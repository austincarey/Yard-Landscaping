# Purple Coneflower

**Scientific Name:** *Echinacea purpurea*
**Type:** Perennial
**Seed Mix:** [[Wildflower Seeds|Colorado Wildflower Mix]] & [[Wildflower Seeds|Monarch Rescue Mix]]
**Native to Colorado:** Yes (US)

**Flower Color:** Purple-Pink
**Bloom Time:** Mid-Summer to Late Summer

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun.
- **Soil:** Adaptable, but prefers well-drained soil.
- **Water:** Low to Moderate. Very drought tolerant once established.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Deadhead for continuous blooms, but leave final flowers in fall to go to seed for winter birds (finches).
- **Fertilizing:** Top-dress with compost in spring.
