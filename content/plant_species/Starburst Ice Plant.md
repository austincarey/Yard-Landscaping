# Starburst Ice Plant

**Scientific Name:** *Delosperma floribundum 'Starburst'*
**Type:** Perennial Succulent
**Native to Colorado:** Plant Select

**Flower Color:** Lilac-purple with white centers
**Bloom Time:** Summer
**Height:** 2 - 4 inches
**Width:** 8 - 20 inches

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun (at least 6-8 hours).
- **Soil:** Excellent drainage is critical (sandy, gravelly).
- **Water:** Highly drought-tolerant once established. Low to moderate water.
- **Planting Guide:** Avoid heavy organic mulches like bark.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Very low maintenance. Clean up in early spring.
- **Other Info:** Foliage may turn burgundy in fall/winter. Ideal for xeriscapes.
