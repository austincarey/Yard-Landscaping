# New England Aster

**Scientific Name:** *Symphyotrichum novae-angliae*
**Type:** Perennial
**Native to Colorado:** Yes

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun.
- **Soil:** Rich, moist soil.
- **Water:** Moderate to High. Do not let soil dry out entirely, which leads to lower leaf drop.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Pinch back stems before mid-July to encourage a bushier habit and more late-season blooms.
- **Fertilizing:** Apply compost in spring.
