# Chinese Snowball

**Scientific Name:** *Viburnum macrocephalum*
**Type:** Shrub
**Native to Colorado:** No

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun to partial shade.
- **Soil:** Well-drained, slightly acidic soil.
- **Water:** Moderate. Needs consistent moisture.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Prune immediately after flowering to maintain shape.
- **Fertilizing:** Apply a balanced, slow-release fertilizer in early spring.
