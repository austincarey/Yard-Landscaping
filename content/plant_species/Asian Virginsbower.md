# Asian Virginsbower

**Scientific Name:** *Clematis terniflora*
**Type:** Vine
**Native to Colorado:** No

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun to partial shade.
- **Soil:** Adaptable, but prefers well-drained soil.
- **Water:** Moderate.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Blooms on new growth. Prune hard in late winter or early spring down to 12 inches.
- **Fertilizing:** Apply balanced fertilizer in early spring.
