# Field Scabious

**Scientific Name:** *Knautia arvensis*
**Type:** Perennial
**Native to Colorado:** No

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun.
- **Soil:** Adaptable, but prefers well-drained soils.
- **Water:** Low to Moderate. Drought tolerant once established.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Deadhead regularly. Can spread aggressively; pull unwanted seedlings in spring.
- **Fertilizing:** Do not over-fertilize, which leads to weak stems.
