# Peony

**Scientific Name:** *Paeonia lactiflora*
**Type:** Perennial
**Native to Colorado:** No

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun (6-8 hours).
- **Soil:** Deep, rich, well-drained soil.
- **Water:** Moderate. Water deeply during dry spells.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Deadhead after flowering. Cut foliage to the ground in late autumn after frost.
- **Fertilizing:** Apply low-nitrogen fertilizer in early spring as shoots emerge.
