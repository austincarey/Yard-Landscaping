# Shrubby Cinquefoil

**Scientific Name:** *Dasiphora fruticosa*
**Type:** Shrub
**Native to Colorado:** Yes

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun to partial shade.
- **Soil:** Highly adaptable, including poor, rocky soils.
- **Water:** Low to Moderate. Very drought tolerant.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Prune in late winter to remove older stems and maintain compact shape.
- **Fertilizing:** Minimal required.
