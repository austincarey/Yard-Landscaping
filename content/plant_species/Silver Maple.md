# Silver Maple

**Scientific Name:** *Acer saccharinum*
**Type:** Tree
**Native to Colorado:** No

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun.
- **Soil:** Adaptable, tolerates wet soils.
- **Water:** Moderate to High.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Prune in late summer or early fall (pruning in late winter causes excessive sap bleeding). Susceptible to branch breakage in snow/ice storms due to weak wood.
- **Fertilizing:** Apply tree fertilizer in early spring.
