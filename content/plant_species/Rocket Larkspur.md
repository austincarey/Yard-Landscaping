# Rocket Larkspur

**Scientific Name:** *Delphinium consolida*
**Type:** Annual
**Seed Mix:** [[Wildflower Seeds|Colorado Wildflower Mix]] & [[Wildflower Seeds|Monarch Rescue Mix]]
**Native to Colorado:** No

**Flower Color:** Blue, Purple, Pink, White
**Bloom Time:** Early to Mid-Summer

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun.
- **Soil:** Prefers rich, well-drained soils.
- **Water:** Moderate.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Self-sows readily. *Note: All parts of the plant are poisonous if ingested, especially to livestock.*
