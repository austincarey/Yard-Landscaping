# Granita Orange Ice Plant

**Scientific Name:** *Delosperma 'PJS02S'*
**Type:** Perennial Succulent
**Native to Colorado:** Plant Select

**Flower Color:** Bright to rusty orange
**Bloom Time:** Late Spring through Fall
**Height:** 1 - 2 inches
**Width:** 10 - 16 inches

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun.
- **Soil:** Very well-drained (sandy, rocky, loamy).
- **Water:** Waterwise, drought-tolerant once established.
- **Planting Guide:** Space 10-16 inches apart.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Very low maintenance. Prune out damaged stems in early spring.
- **Other Info:** Excellent groundcover. Attracts pollinators.
