# European Cranberry Bush

**Scientific Name:** *Viburnum opulus*
**Type:** Shrub
**Native to Colorado:** No

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun to partial shade.
- **Soil:** Consistently moist, well-drained loam.
- **Water:** Moderate to High. Do not let dry out completely.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Prune immediately after flowering to shape. Thin out old wood every few years.
- **Fertilizing:** Apply a balanced fertilizer in early spring.
