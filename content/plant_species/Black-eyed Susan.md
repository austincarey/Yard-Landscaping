# Black-eyed Susan

**Scientific Name:** *Rudbeckia hirta*
**Type:** Biennial/Perennial
**Seed Mix:** [[Wildflower Seeds|Colorado Wildflower Mix]]
**Native to Colorado:** Yes

**Flower Color:** Golden Yellow with dark center
**Bloom Time:** Mid-Summer to Fall

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun.
- **Soil:** Adaptable to poor soils.
- **Water:** Moderate.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Deadhead to prolong blooming. Leave late season flowers for seed heads.
- **Fertilizing:** Minimal.
