# Lance-leaved Coreopsis

**Scientific Name:** *Coreopsis lanceolata*
**Type:** Perennial
**Seed Mix:** [[Wildflower Seeds|Colorado Wildflower Mix]]
**Native to Colorado:** Yes (US)

**Flower Color:** Yellow
**Bloom Time:** Late Spring to Mid-Summer

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun.
- **Soil:** Dry, rocky, or sandy well-drained soils.
- **Water:** Low.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Deadhead to prolong blooming and prevent excessive self-seeding.
- **Fertilizing:** None needed.
