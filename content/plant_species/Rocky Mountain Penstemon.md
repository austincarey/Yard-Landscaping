# Rocky Mountain Penstemon

**Scientific Name:** *Penstemon strictus*
**Type:** Perennial
**Seed Mix:** [[Wildflower Seeds|Colorado Wildflower Mix]]
**Native to Colorado:** Yes

**Flower Color:** Deep Blue to Purple
**Bloom Time:** Late Spring to Early Summer

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun.
- **Soil:** Gravelly or sandy well-drained soils. Intolerant of wet clay.
- **Water:** Low. Drought tolerant native.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Cut back flower stalks after blooming. Cut foliage to ground in late winter.
- **Fertilizing:** Do not fertilize.
