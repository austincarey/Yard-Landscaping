# Pearly Everlasting

**Scientific Name:** *Anaphalis margaritacea*
**Type:** Perennial
**Native to Colorado:** Yes

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun to partial shade.
- **Soil:** Dry, rocky, or sandy well-drained soils.
- **Water:** Low. Excellent drought tolerance. Ideal for xeriscaping.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Harvest flowers in late summer for dried arrangements. Cut foliage to the ground in late autumn.
- **Fertilizing:** None needed. Thrives in poor soil.
