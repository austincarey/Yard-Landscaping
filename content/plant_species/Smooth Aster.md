# Smooth Aster

**Scientific Name:** *Symphyotrichum laeve*
**Type:** Perennial
**Seed Mix:** [[Wildflower Seeds|Monarch Rescue Mix]]
**Native to Colorado:** Yes

**Flower Color:** Light Blue to Violet
**Bloom Time:** Late Summer to Fall

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun to partial shade.
- **Soil:** Adaptable to a wide range of soils.
- **Water:** Low to moderate.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Can be pinched back in early summer to encourage bushier growth and more blooms in the fall. Important late-season nectar source.
