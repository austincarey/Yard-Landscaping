# Carrot

**Scientific Name:** *Daucus carota subsp. sativus*
**Type:** Biennial Crop
**Native to Colorado:** No

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun.
- **Soil:** Deep, loose, well-drained soil. Heavy clay causes stunted or forked roots.
- **Water:** Moderate. Consistent moisture is key for good root development.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Thin seedlings early to ensure adequate space.
- **Fertilizing:** Avoid high nitrogen fertilizers which cause hairy roots.
