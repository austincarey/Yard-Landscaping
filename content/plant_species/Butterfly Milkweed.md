# Butterfly Milkweed

**Scientific Name:** *Asclepias tuberosa*
**Type:** Perennial
**Seed Mix:** [[Wildflower Seeds|Monarch Rescue Mix]]
**Native to Colorado:** Yes

**Flower Color:** Bright Orange
**Bloom Time:** Mid-Summer to Late Summer

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun.
- **Soil:** Well-drained, sandy or gravelly soils.
- **Water:** Low. Very drought tolerant due to its deep taproot.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Slow to emerge in spring. Leave seed pods for visual interest or remove to prevent spreading. Essential host plant for Monarch butterflies.
