# Gayfeather

**Scientific Name:** *Liatris spicata*
**Type:** Perennial
**Seed Mix:** [[Wildflower Seeds|Monarch Rescue Mix]]
**Native to Colorado:** Yes (Some species)

**Flower Color:** Purple
**Bloom Time:** Late Summer to Fall

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun.
- **Soil:** Adaptable, performs well in poor soils.
- **Water:** Low to moderate. Drought tolerant once established.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Leave flower stalks in winter for birds.
