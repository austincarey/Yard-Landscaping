# Indian Blanket

**Scientific Name:** *Gaillardia pulchella*
**Type:** Annual
**Seed Mix:** [[Wildflower Seeds|Colorado Wildflower Mix]]
**Native to Colorado:** Yes (US)

**Flower Color:** Red with Yellow tips
**Bloom Time:** Early Summer to Fall

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun.
- **Soil:** Dry, sandy or rocky well-drained soils.
- **Water:** Very Low. Drought tolerant.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Deadhead to prolong blooming.
- **Fertilizing:** None needed.
