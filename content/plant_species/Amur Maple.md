# Amur Maple

**Scientific Name:** *Acer ginnala*
**Type:** Tree/Shrub
**Native to Colorado:** No

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun to partial shade.
- **Soil:** Adaptable, including alkaline CO soils.
- **Water:** Moderate. Drought tolerant once established.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Prune in late fall or winter when dormant to shape.
- **Fertilizing:** Apply tree fertilizer in early spring.
