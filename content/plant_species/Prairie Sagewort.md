# Prairie Sagewort

**Scientific Name:** *Artemisia frigida*
**Type:** Perennial
**Native to Colorado:** Yes

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun.
- **Soil:** Dry, sandy, or rocky soils. Cannot tolerate wet winter soils.
- **Water:** Very Low. Extremely drought tolerant native.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Cut back hard in early spring to maintain a compact mound and prevent woody centers.
- **Fertilizing:** Do not fertilize.
