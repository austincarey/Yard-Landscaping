# Garden Lettuce

**Scientific Name:** *Lactuca sativa*
**Type:** Annual Crop
**Native to Colorado:** No

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Partial shade. Prefers cooler temperatures; bolts in high summer heat.
- **Soil:** Rich, moisture-retentive soil.
- **Water:** Moderate to High. Consistent moisture prevents bitterness.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Harvest outer leaves continuously, or harvest entire heads.
- **Fertilizing:** Apply nitrogen-rich vegetable fertilizer.
