# Serbian Bellflower

**Scientific Name:** *Campanula poscharskyana*
**Type:** Perennial
**Native to Colorado:** No

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun to partial shade. Benefits from afternoon shade in hot Colorado summers.
- **Soil:** Moist but well-drained. Tolerates rocky or sandy soil.
- **Water:** Moderate. Needs consistent moisture, do not let it dry out completely.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Shear back aggressively after the initial summer bloom to encourage a potential second, lighter autumn bloom and tidy appearance.
- **Fertilizing:** Top-dress with compost in spring. Liquid fertilizer mid-summer can boost secondary blooming.
