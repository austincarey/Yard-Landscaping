# Elm-leaf Spiraea

**Scientific Name:** *Spiraea chamaedryfolia*
**Type:** Shrub
**Native to Colorado:** No

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun for best flowering, tolerates partial shade.
- **Soil:** Adaptable to a wide range of soils, including clay, if not completely waterlogged.
- **Water:** Moderate. Water deeply during severe drought.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Prune immediately after spring flowering to shape. Remove 1/3 of older stems to the ground every few years for rejuvenation.
- **Fertilizing:** Apply a balanced shrub fertilizer in early spring before bud break.
