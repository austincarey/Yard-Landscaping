# Lemon Balm

**Scientific Name:** *Melissa officinalis*
**Type:** Perennial Herb
**Native to Colorado:** No

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun to partial shade.
- **Soil:** Adaptable.
- **Water:** Moderate.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Cut back aggressively after blooming to prevent rampant self-seeding.
- **Fertilizing:** Minimal.
