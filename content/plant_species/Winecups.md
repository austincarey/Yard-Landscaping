# Winecups

**Scientific Name:** *Callirhoe involucrata*
**Type:** Perennial
**Native to Colorado:** Native / Plant Select

**Flower Color:** Magenta
**Bloom Time:** Summer
**Height:** 3 - 12 inches
**Width:** 1.5 - 3 feet

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun.
- **Soil:** Well-drained.
- **Water:** Drought-tolerant (deep taproot).
- **Planting Guide:** Protect from rabbits until established. Difficult to transplant later.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** May go dormant in extreme heat without water.
- **Other Info:** Excellent trailing habit.

# Plant Select Notes
# Winecups (aka, Purple Poppy Mallow)

Callirhoe involucrata

![Winecups | aka, Purple Poppy Mallow | Plant Select | Callirhoe involucrata](https://plantselect.org/plant_image/callirhoe-involucrata.jpg)

**Winecups (aka, purple poppy mallow)** is a native plant that thrives at the front of the border, in the wild garden and in xeriscapes where it can enjoy the full sun. It blooms with wine-purple flowers from late spring to hard frost. Tolerates a wide range of soils. Perennial. Xeriscape.  
  
**Tips from the pros:** Less water and some pinching back will help this plant stay more compact and keep it from spreading as wide. It does self-seed, so remove any seedlings when they are young, before the roots get a good foothold.
