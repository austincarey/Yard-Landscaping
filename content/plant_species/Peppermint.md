# Peppermint

**Scientific Name:** *Mentha x piperita*
**Type:** Perennial Herb
**Native to Colorado:** No

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun to partial shade.
- **Soil:** Rich, moist soil.
- **Water:** Moderate to High.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Harvest leaves frequently. Extremely invasive root system; strongly recommended to grow in pots.
- **Fertilizing:** Liquid fertilizer mid-season if grown in pots.
