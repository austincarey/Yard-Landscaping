# Pikes Peak Purple Penstemon

**Scientific Name:** *Penstemon x mexicali 'Pike's Peak Purple'*
**Type:** Perennial
**Native to Colorado:** Plant Select

**Flower Color:** Violet-purple
**Bloom Time:** Summer
**Height:** 12 - 18 inches
**Width:** 10 - 15 inches

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun.
- **Soil:** Excellent drainage is critical.
- **Water:** Moderate water needs.
- **Planting Guide:** Bushy, clumping habit.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Remove old/dead foliage in late winter or early spring.
- **Other Info:** Attracts pollinators. Deer and rabbit resistant.
