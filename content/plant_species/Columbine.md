# Columbine

**Scientific Name:** *Aquilegia coerulea*
**Type:** Perennial
**Seed Mix:** [[Wildflower Seeds|Colorado Wildflower Mix]]
**Native to Colorado:** Yes (Colorado State Flower)

**Flower Color:** Blue and White
**Bloom Time:** Mid-Spring to Early Summer

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Partial shade. Protect from hot afternoon sun.
- **Soil:** Rich, consistently moist, well-drained soil.
- **Water:** Moderate. Do not let soil dry out completely.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Deadhead to encourage more blooms, or leave seed heads for self-sowing. Leaf miners commonly damage leaves; cut off damaged foliage.
- **Fertilizing:** Top-dress with compost in spring.
