# Weigela

**Scientific Name:** *Weigela florida*
**Type:** Shrub
**Native to Colorado:** No

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun.
- **Soil:** Well-drained soil.
- **Water:** Moderate.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Prune immediately after spring flowering. Remove oldest branches to the ground to rejuvenate.
- **Fertilizing:** Apply balanced fertilizer in early spring.
