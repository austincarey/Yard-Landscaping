# Garden Cosmos

**Scientific Name:** *Cosmos bipinnatus*
**Type:** Annual
**Seed Mix:** [[Wildflower Seeds|Colorado Wildflower Mix]] & [[Wildflower Seeds|Monarch Rescue Mix]]
**Native to Colorado:** No

**Flower Color:** Pink, White, Magenta
**Bloom Time:** Mid-Summer to Fall

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun.
- **Soil:** Average to poor, well-drained soil.
- **Water:** Low. Drought tolerant.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Deadhead to encourage continuous blooming. Tall plants may need staking.
- **Fertilizing:** Do not fertilize. Rich soil produces weak stems and fewer flowers.
