# Daylily

**Scientific Name:** *Hemerocallis*
**Type:** Perennial
**Native to Colorado:** No

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun to partial shade.
- **Soil:** Adaptable to almost any well-drained soil.
- **Water:** Moderate. Tolerates drought but blooms better with regular water.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Remove spent scapes (flower stalks) after blooming. Divide clumps in early spring or fall every 3-4 years.
- **Fertilizing:** Apply balanced fertilizer in early spring.
