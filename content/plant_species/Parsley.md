# Parsley

**Scientific Name:** *Petroselinum crispum*
**Type:** Biennial Herb
**Native to Colorado:** No

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun to partial shade.
- **Soil:** Rich, moist soil.
- **Water:** Moderate to High.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Harvest outer stems at the base continuously.
- **Fertilizing:** Apply liquid fertilizer every 3-4 weeks.
