# Trumpet Vine

**Scientific Name:** *Campsis radicans*
**Type:** Vine
**Native to Colorado:** No (Native to Eastern US)

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun for best flowering.
- **Soil:** Highly adaptable to poor soils.
- **Water:** Low to Moderate. Drought tolerant once established.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Extremely vigorous and aggressive. Prune back heavily in late winter or early spring to control size. Pull up root suckers promptly.
- **Fertilizing:** Avoid fertilizing, which promotes excessive leafy growth over flowers.
