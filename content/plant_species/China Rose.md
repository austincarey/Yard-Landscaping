# China Rose

**Scientific Name:** *Rosa chinensis*
**Type:** Shrub
**Native to Colorado:** No

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun.
- **Soil:** Rich, loamy, well-drained soil. Amend heavy clay.
- **Water:** Moderate to High. Water at the base to prevent powdery mildew on leaves.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Major pruning in early spring just as buds swell. Remove dead/crossing branches. Deadhead throughout summer.
- **Fertilizing:** Heavy feeders. Apply rose fertilizer every 4-6 weeks during the growing season.
