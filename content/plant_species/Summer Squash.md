# Summer Squash

**Scientific Name:** *Cucurbita pepo*
**Type:** Annual Crop
**Native to Colorado:** No

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun (8+ hours).
- **Soil:** Rich, well-draining soil heavily amended with compost.
- **Water:** High. Consistent moisture is critical, especially when fruiting. Water at the base to prevent powdery mildew.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Harvest fruits frequently to encourage continuous production.
- **Fertilizing:** Heavy feeders. Apply vegetable fertilizer monthly.
