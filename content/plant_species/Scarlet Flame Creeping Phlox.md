# Scarlet Flame Creeping Phlox

**Scientific Name:** *Phlox subulata 'Scarlet Flame'*
**Type:** Perennial (Evergreen)
**Native to Colorado:** No

**Flower Color:** Red/Scarlet
**Bloom Time:** Spring
**Height:** 4 - 6 inches
**Width:** 12 - 24 inches

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun.
- **Soil:** Well-drained (gritty, sandy, or rocky).
- **Water:** Drought-tolerant once established.
- **Planting Guide:** Does not tolerate standing water.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Shear foliage back by half after blooming to maintain shape.
- **Other Info:** Mat-forming groundcover. Deer and rabbit resistant.
