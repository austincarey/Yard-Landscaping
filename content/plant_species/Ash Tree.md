# Ash Tree

**Scientific Name:** *Fraxinus*
**Type:** Tree
**Native to Colorado:** Some species yes

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun.
- **Soil:** Adaptable.
- **Water:** Moderate.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Prune during winter dormancy. **CRITICAL:** High risk of Emerald Ash Borer. Consult an arborist for systemic preventative treatments.
- **Fertilizing:** Apply tree fertilizer in early spring.
