# Basket of Gold

**Scientific Name:** *Aurinia saxatilis*
**Type:** Perennial
**Native to Colorado:** No

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun.
- **Soil:** Requires excellent drainage. Gravelly or rocky soils are best. Prone to root rot in heavy clay.
- **Water:** Low. Highly drought tolerant.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Cut back by 1/3 immediately after spring flowering to maintain tight shape.
- **Fertilizing:** None needed.
