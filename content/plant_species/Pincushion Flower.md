# Pincushion Flower

**Scientific Name:** *Scabiosa columbaria*
**Type:** Perennial
**Native to Colorado:** No

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun. Prefers intense afternoon sun.
- **Soil:** Well-drained, average to rich soil. Prone to crown rot in poorly drained winter soils.
- **Water:** Moderate. Needs regular watering during the first growing season. Moderately drought-tolerant thereafter.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Deadhead spent blooms religiously to promote continuous flowering through summer.
- **Fertilizing:** Apply a balanced slow-release fertilizer in early spring.
