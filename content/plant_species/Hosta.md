# Hosta

**Scientific Name:** *Hosta spp.*
**Type:** Perennial
**Native to Colorado:** No

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full shade to partial shade.
- **Soil:** Rich, well-draining, slightly acidic soil.
- **Water:** High water needs. Requires consistent moisture.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Cut back dead foliage after the first frost.
- **Other Info:** Two 1 GPH drip emitters provide deep soaking needed for their root structure.
