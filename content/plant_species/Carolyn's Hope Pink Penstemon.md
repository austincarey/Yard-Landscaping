# Carolyn's Hope Pink Penstemon

**Scientific Name:** *Penstemon x mexicali 'Carolyn's Hope'*
**Type:** Perennial
**Native to Colorado:** Plant Select

**Flower Color:** Pink with white throat
**Bloom Time:** Summer
**Height:** 12 - 18 inches
**Width:** 12 - 14 inches

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun to partial shade.
- **Soil:** Well-drained.
- **Water:** Moderate to low water. Drought-tolerant.
- **Planting Guide:** Do not plant in standing water.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Leave foliage over winter, cut back dead stems in early spring.
- **Other Info:** Attracts bees, butterflies, and hummingbirds.
