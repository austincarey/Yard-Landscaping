# Spotted Knapweed

**Scientific Name:** *Centaurea stoebe*
**Type:** Weed (Invasive)
**Native to Colorado:** No

## Weed Extermination
Hand pull small patches wearing gloves (can irritate skin). Use broadleaf herbicides (aminopyralid, clopyralid) during rosette stage.
