# Western Blue Virginsbower

**Scientific Name:** *Clematis occidentalis*
**Type:** Vine
**Native to Colorado:** Yes

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Roots in shade, tops in sun. Provide a thick layer of mulch.
- **Soil:** Rich, moist, well-drained soil.
- **Water:** Moderate to High. Requires consistent moisture.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Prune lightly in late winter or early spring to remove dead or weak stems. Blooms on old wood.
- **Fertilizing:** Apply balanced fertilizer in early spring.
