# California Poppy

**Scientific Name:** *Eschscholzia californica*
**Type:** Annual / Tender Perennial
**Seed Mix:** [[Wildflower Seeds|Colorado Wildflower Mix]]
**Native to Colorado:** No (Native to West Coast, widely naturalized)

**Flower Color:** Golden Orange
**Bloom Time:** Spring to Early Summer

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun.
- **Soil:** Poor, well-drained soils.
- **Water:** Low. Very drought tolerant.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Freely self-seeds to come back year after year. Close their flowers at night and on cloudy days.
