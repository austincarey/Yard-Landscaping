# Baby Blue-eyes

**Scientific Name:** *Nemophila menziesii*
**Type:** Annual
**Seed Mix:** [[Wildflower Seeds|Colorado Wildflower Mix]]
**Native to Colorado:** No (Native to West Coast)

**Flower Color:** Sky Blue with white center
**Bloom Time:** Spring to Early Summer

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun to partial shade.
- **Soil:** Prefers well-drained, somewhat sandy soils.
- **Water:** Moderate. Enjoys consistent moisture but will rot in soggy soil.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Excellent low-growing edge plant or ground cover.
