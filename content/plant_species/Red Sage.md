# Red Sage

**Scientific Name:** *Salvia spp. (e.g., Salvia darcyi or Salvia splendens)*
**Type:** Perennial (often grown as annual depending on hardiness)
**Native to Colorado:** No

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun.
- **Soil:** Well-draining soil.
- **Water:** Low water needs (Xeric). Prefers to stay on the drier side to avoid root rot.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Deadhead spent blooms to encourage further flowering.
- **Other Info:** Setup with one 0.5 GPH emitter. Highly drought-tolerant once established.
