# Kentucky Bluegrass

**Scientific Name:** *Poa pratensis*
**Type:** Grass
**Native to Colorado:** No

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun.
- **Soil:** Rich, well-drained soil.
- **Water:** High. Requires 1.5 - 2 inches of water per week in peak summer heat.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Mow at 2.5 - 3 inches. Aerate in fall.
- **Fertilizing:** Fertilize heavily in fall (September/October/November) and lightly in late spring.
