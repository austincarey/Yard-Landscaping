# Shirley Poppy

**Scientific Name:** *Papaver rhoeas*
**Type:** Annual
**Seed Mix:** [[Wildflower Seeds|Colorado Wildflower Mix]]
**Native to Colorado:** No

**Flower Color:** Red, Pink, White
**Bloom Time:** Late Spring to Mid-Summer

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun.
- **Soil:** Well-drained, average soils.
- **Water:** Moderate.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Allow seed pods to develop and open to ensure self-seeding for next year.
