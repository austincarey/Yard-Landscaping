# Red Oak

**Scientific Name:** *Quercus rubra*
**Type:** Tree
**Native to Colorado:** No

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun.
- **Soil:** Prefers acidic soils. Highly susceptible to iron chlorosis in Colorado's alkaline soils.
- **Water:** Moderate.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Prune only in winter dormancy to reduce risk of Oak Wilt.
- **Fertilizing:** May require iron chelate soil injections if leaves turn yellow with green veins (chlorosis).
