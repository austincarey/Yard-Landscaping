# Pine Tree

**Scientific Name:** *Pinus*
**Type:** Tree
**Native to Colorado:** Some species yes

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun.
- **Soil:** Adaptable, well-drained soils.
- **Water:** Low to Moderate. Very drought tolerant once established.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Prune 'candles' (new growth) in spring to control size or encourage density. Remove dead lower branches.
- **Fertilizing:** Usually not required.
