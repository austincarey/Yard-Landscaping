# Annual Candytuft

**Scientific Name:** *Iberis umbellata*
**Type:** Annual
**Seed Mix:** [[Wildflower Seeds|Colorado Wildflower Mix]] & [[Wildflower Seeds|Monarch Rescue Mix]]
**Native to Colorado:** No

**Flower Color:** Pink, Purple, White
**Bloom Time:** Late Spring to Mid-Summer

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun to partial shade.
- **Soil:** Well-drained.
- **Water:** Moderate.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Can reseed itself lightly. Great for quick seasonal color on borders.
