# Whitetop

**Scientific Name:** *Lepidium draba*
**Type:** Weed (Invasive)
**Native to Colorado:** No

## Weed Extermination
Systemic herbicides (metsulfuron, chlorsulfuron) applied at rosette to early bud stage. Mowing alone is ineffective.
