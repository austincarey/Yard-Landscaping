# Hoary Vervain

**Scientific Name:** *Verbena stricta*
**Type:** Perennial
**Seed Mix:** [[Wildflower Seeds|Monarch Rescue Mix]]
**Native to Colorado:** Yes

**Flower Color:** Blue to Purple
**Bloom Time:** Mid-Summer to Fall

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun.
- **Soil:** Dry, well-drained soil; very tolerant of poor, sandy, or clay soils.
- **Water:** Very low. Highly drought tolerant.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Can spread via self-seeding. Great for late-season pollinators.
