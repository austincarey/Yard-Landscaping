# California Brittlebrush

**Scientific Name:** *Encelia californica*
**Type:** Shrub
**Native to Colorado:** No (Native to CA)

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun. Requires intense heat.
- **Soil:** Sandy, rocky, excellent drainage.
- **Water:** Very Low. Needs very little supplemental water.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Cut back aggressively after flowering to prevent woody, leggy growth.
- **Fertilizing:** None needed.
