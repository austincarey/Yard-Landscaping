# Blue Flax

**Scientific Name:** *Linum perenne*
**Type:** Perennial
**Seed Mix:** [[Wildflower Seeds|Colorado Wildflower Mix]]
**Native to Colorado:** Yes

**Flower Color:** Sky Blue
**Bloom Time:** Late Spring to Mid-Summer

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun to partial shade.
- **Soil:** Well-drained soil; very adaptable to poor soils.
- **Water:** Low. Drought tolerant once established.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Self-sows readily. Cut back in late fall or early spring.
