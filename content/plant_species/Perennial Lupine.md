# Perennial Lupine

**Scientific Name:** *Lupinus perennis*
**Type:** Perennial
**Seed Mix:** [[Wildflower Seeds|Colorado Wildflower Mix]]
**Native to Colorado:** Yes

**Flower Color:** Blue/Purple
**Bloom Time:** Late Spring to Early Summer

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun to partial shade.
- **Soil:** Prefers well-drained, slightly acidic to neutral soil. Does well in poor, sandy soils.
- **Water:** Low to moderate.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Deadhead spent flowers to encourage more blooms and prevent unwanted self-seeding, or leave for naturalizing. Fixes nitrogen in the soil.
