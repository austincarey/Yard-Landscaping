# Columbine Green Apples

**Scientific Name:** *Aquilegia clematiflora 'Green Apples'*
**Type:** Perennial
**Native to Colorado:** No

**Flower Color:** Apple-green to creamy white
**Bloom Time:** Spring to Early Summer
**Height:** 18 - 36 inches
**Width:** 12 - 24 inches

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun to partial shade (can tolerate full shade).
- **Soil:** Adaptable (fertile, well-drained).
- **Water:** Even moisture, do not let dry out completely.
- **Planting Guide:** Space 12-24 inches apart.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Deadhead for more blooms. Cut back to ground mid-summer if foliage declines.
- **Other Info:** Self-seeds prolifically. Attracts pollinators, ignored by deer/rabbits.
