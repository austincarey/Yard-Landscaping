# Japanese Knotweed

**Scientific Name:** *Reynoutria japonica*
**Type:** Weed (Highly Invasive)
**Native to Colorado:** No

## Weed Extermination
DO NOT cut or mow (spreads via fragments). Use systemic herbicides (glyphosate/triclopyr) applied to freshly cut stems or sprayed on foliage in late summer/fall. Digging is ineffective.
