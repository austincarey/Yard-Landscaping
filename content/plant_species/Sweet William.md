# Sweet William

**Scientific Name:** *Dianthus barbatus*
**Type:** Biennial/Perennial
**Seed Mix:** [[Wildflower Seeds|Colorado Wildflower Mix]]
**Native to Colorado:** No

**Flower Color:** Pink, Red, White, Bicolor
**Bloom Time:** Late Spring to Early Summer

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun to partial shade.
- **Soil:** Rich, well-drained, slightly alkaline soil.
- **Water:** Moderate.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Deadhead immediately after blooming to encourage a second flush and perennation.
- **Fertilizing:** Apply balanced fertilizer in early spring.
