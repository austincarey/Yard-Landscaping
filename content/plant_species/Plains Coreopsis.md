# Plains Coreopsis

**Scientific Name:** *Coreopsis tinctoria*
**Type:** Annual
**Seed Mix:** [[Wildflower Seeds|Colorado Wildflower Mix]]
**Native to Colorado:** Yes

**Flower Color:** Yellow with maroon center
**Bloom Time:** Mid-Summer to Fall

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun.
- **Soil:** Adaptable to poor soils.
- **Water:** Low. Drought tolerant native.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Reseeds easily. Pull unwanted seedlings.
- **Fertilizing:** None needed.
