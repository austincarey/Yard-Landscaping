# Catchfly

**Scientific Name:** *Silene armeria*
**Type:** Annual
**Seed Mix:** [[Wildflower Seeds|Colorado Wildflower Mix]]
**Native to Colorado:** No

**Flower Color:** Bright Pink/Magenta
**Bloom Time:** Mid to Late Summer

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun to partial shade.
- **Soil:** Adaptable, can tolerate poor soils as long as they drain well.
- **Water:** Low to moderate.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Readily self-sows. Very vibrant pink blooms that add intense pops of color.
