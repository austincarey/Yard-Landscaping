# Zinnia

**Scientific Name:** *Zinnia elegans*
**Type:** Annual
**Seed Mix:** [[Wildflower Seeds|Monarch Rescue Mix]]
**Native to Colorado:** No

**Flower Color:** Multicolored (Pink, Red, Yellow, Orange)
**Bloom Time:** Mid-Summer to Fall

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun.
- **Soil:** Well-drained, fertile soil.
- **Water:** Moderate. Water at the base to avoid powdery mildew on leaves.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Deadhead regularly to promote continuous blooming until frost.
