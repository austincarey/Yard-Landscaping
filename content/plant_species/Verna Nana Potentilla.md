# Verna Nana Potentilla

**Scientific Name:** *Potentilla neumanniana 'Nana'*
**Type:** Perennial
**Native to Colorado:** No

**Flower Color:** Yellow
**Bloom Time:** Spring
**Height:** 2 - 4 inches
**Width:** 12 inches or more

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun to partial shade.
- **Soil:** Well-drained, highly adaptable.
- **Water:** Tolerant of dry to moderately moist conditions.
- **Planting Guide:** Great for rock gardens or between stepping stones.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Prune only after flowering. Can divide in spring or fall.
- **Other Info:** Deer resistant and attracts pollinators.
