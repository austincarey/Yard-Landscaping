# Granita Raspberry Ice Plant

**Scientific Name:** *Delosperma 'PJS01S'*
**Type:** Perennial Succulent
**Native to Colorado:** Plant Select

**Flower Color:** Raspberry/Magenta
**Bloom Time:** Summer
**Height:** 1 - 4 inches
**Width:** 14 - 30 inches

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun.
- **Soil:** Excellent drainage is critical.
- **Water:** Drought-tolerant once established. Allow soil to dry out between waterings.
- **Planting Guide:** Mulch with small gravel to prevent rot in wet winters.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Trim to shape or remove dead stems in early spring.
- **Other Info:** Mat-forming groundcover. Deer resistant.
