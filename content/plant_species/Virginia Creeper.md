# Virginia Creeper

**Scientific Name:** *Parthenocissus quinquefolia*
**Type:** Vine
**Native to Colorado:** Yes

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun to full shade. Best fall color in full sun.
- **Soil:** Adaptable to almost any soil.
- **Water:** Moderate. Drought tolerant once established.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Extremely fast grower. Prune back aggressively as needed to keep it off gutters and siding.
- **Fertilizing:** None needed.
