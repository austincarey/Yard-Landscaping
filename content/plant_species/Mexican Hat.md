# Mexican Hat

**Scientific Name:** *Ratibida columnifera* (Mexican Hat variety)
**Type:** Perennial
**Seed Mix:** [[Wildflower Seeds|Colorado Wildflower Mix]]
**Native to Colorado:** Yes

**Flower Color:** Red/Mahogany with yellow edges
**Bloom Time:** Mid-Summer to Late Summer

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun.
- **Soil:** Thrives in well-drained soils, tolerates clay and poor, dry conditions.
- **Water:** Low. Very drought tolerant.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Leave seedheads standing through winter for birds and natural reseeding.
