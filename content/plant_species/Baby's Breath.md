# Baby's Breath

**Scientific Name:** *Gypsophila elegans*
**Type:** Annual
**Seed Mix:** [[Wildflower Seeds|Colorado Wildflower Mix]]
**Native to Colorado:** No

**Flower Color:** White
**Bloom Time:** Early to Mid-Summer

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun.
- **Soil:** Well-drained, alkaline preferred.
- **Water:** Low to moderate.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Fast-growing but short bloom time. Successive sowing can prolong the display.
