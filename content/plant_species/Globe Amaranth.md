# Globe Amaranth

**Scientific Name:** *Gomphrena globosa*
**Type:** Annual
**Native to Colorado:** No

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun. Thrives in heat.
- **Soil:** Tolerant of poor soils, but must have good drainage.
- **Water:** Low. Very drought tolerant. Allow soil to dry out between waterings.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Pinch back young plants to encourage bushiness. Flowers are excellent for cutting and drying.
- **Fertilizing:** Minimal. Too much nitrogen reduces flowering.
