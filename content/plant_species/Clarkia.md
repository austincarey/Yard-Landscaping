# Clarkia

**Scientific Name:** *Clarkia unguiculata*
**Type:** Annual
**Seed Mix:** [[Wildflower Seeds|Colorado Wildflower Mix]]
**Native to Colorado:** No

**Flower Color:** Pink, Purple, Salmon, White
**Bloom Time:** Late Spring to Mid-Summer

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun.
- **Soil:** Well-drained, sandy or loamy soils.
- **Water:** Low to moderate. Drought tolerant once established.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Easy to grow from seed; provides a nice vertical element in mixed flower beds.
