# Bloody Cranesbill

**Scientific Name:** *Geranium sanguineum*
**Type:** Perennial
**Native to Colorado:** No

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun to partial shade.
- **Soil:** Well-drained soil. Tolerates drought once established.
- **Water:** Moderate.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Shear back in mid-summer if foliage becomes leggy.
- **Fertilizing:** Top-dress with compost in spring.
