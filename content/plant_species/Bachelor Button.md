# Bachelor Button

**Scientific Name:** *Centaurea cyanus*
**Type:** Annual
**Seed Mix:** [[Wildflower Seeds|Colorado Wildflower Mix]]
**Native to Colorado:** No

**Flower Color:** Blue, Pink, White
**Bloom Time:** Early Summer to Fall

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun.
- **Soil:** Adaptable, well-drained.
- **Water:** Moderate.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Readily self-seeds. Deadhead to prolong flowering period.
