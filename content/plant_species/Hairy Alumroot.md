# Hairy Alumroot

**Scientific Name:** *Heuchera villosa*
**Type:** Perennial
**Native to Colorado:** No

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Partial to full shade. Too much sun will scorch the leaves.
- **Soil:** Rich, moist, well-drained soil. Intolerant of heavy clay.
- **Water:** Moderate to High. Requires consistently moist soil.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Remove damaged or tattered foliage in early spring before new growth begins.
- **Fertilizing:** Feed lightly in early spring with an organic granular fertilizer.
