# Columbine 'Winky Double Red-White'

**Scientific Name:** *Aquilegia vulgaris 'Winky Double Red and White'*
**Type:** Perennial
**Native to Colorado:** No

**Flower Color:** Red and White
**Bloom Time:** Late Spring to Early Summer
**Height:** 12 - 14 inches
**Width:** 10 - 15 inches

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun to part shade (afternoon shade in hot climates).
- **Soil:** Average, medium moisture, well-drained.
- **Water:** Consistent moisture until established.
- **Planting Guide:** Space 10-15 inches apart.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Deadhead for more blooms. Cut foliage back after decline in midsummer.
- **Other Info:** Compact, bushy habit. Self-seeds. Attracts hummingbirds and butterflies. Deer/rabbit resistant.
