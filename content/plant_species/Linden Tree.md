# Linden Tree

**Scientific Name:** *Tilia*
**Type:** Tree
**Native to Colorado:** No

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun to partial shade.
- **Soil:** Rich, moist, well-drained loam.
- **Water:** Moderate. Does not tolerate severe drought well.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Prune in late winter or early spring while dormant. Often develops basal suckers which should be removed.
- **Fertilizing:** Apply tree fertilizer in early spring.
