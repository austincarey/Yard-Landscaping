# Moss Phlox

**Scientific Name:** *Phlox subulata*
**Type:** Perennial
**Native to Colorado:** No

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun. Needs 6+ hours of direct sunlight for best blooming.
- **Soil:** Sandy, rocky, or well-drained soils. Intolerant of heavy, wet clay.
- **Water:** Low to moderate. Very drought tolerant once established. Water lightly during extended dry spells.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Shear back foliage by 1/2 immediately after spring flowering to maintain a dense, compact habit and stimulate new growth.
- **Fertilizing:** Seldom needed. Light compost application in early spring is sufficient.
