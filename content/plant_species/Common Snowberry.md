# Common Snowberry

**Scientific Name:** *Symphoricarpos albus*
**Type:** Shrub
**Native to Colorado:** Yes

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Adaptable from full sun to heavy shade.
- **Soil:** Adaptable to wide range of soils including clay and rocky sites.
- **Water:** Low to Moderate. Drought tolerant native.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Prune in late winter or early spring. Can be cut back to the ground if it becomes too leggy.
- **Fertilizing:** None generally required.
