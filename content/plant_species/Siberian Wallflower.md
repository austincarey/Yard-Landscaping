# Siberian Wallflower

**Scientific Name:** *Cheiranthus allionii* (or *Erysimum x allionii*)
**Type:** Biennial / Short-lived Perennial
**Seed Mix:** [[Wildflower Seeds|Colorado Wildflower Mix]] & [[Wildflower Seeds|Monarch Rescue Mix]]
**Native to Colorado:** No (Introduced)

**Flower Color:** Bright Orange
**Bloom Time:** Spring to Early Summer

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun.
- **Soil:** Well-drained. Tolerates poor soils.
- **Water:** Low to moderate.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Readily reseeds to act like a perennial. Pull dead plants after they have dropped seed.
