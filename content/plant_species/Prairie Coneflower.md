# Prairie Coneflower

**Scientific Name:** *Ratibida columnifera*
**Type:** Perennial
**Seed Mix:** [[Wildflower Seeds|Colorado Wildflower Mix]]
**Native to Colorado:** Yes

**Flower Color:** Yellow with brown center
**Bloom Time:** Mid-Summer to Late Summer

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun.
- **Soil:** Highly adaptable, thrives in well-drained, dry soils including clay and loam.
- **Water:** Very low. Highly drought tolerant.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Leave seedheads for winter bird food. Cut back in early spring.
