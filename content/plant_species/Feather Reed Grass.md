# Feather Reed Grass

**Scientific Name:** *Calamagrostis x acutiflora*
**Type:** Grass
**Native to Colorado:** No

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun.
- **Soil:** Heavy clay to well-drained sand.
- **Water:** Moderate. Tolerates wet soils better than many ornamental grasses.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Cut clumps to the ground (2-3 inches high) in late winter or early spring before new growth begins.
- **Fertilizing:** Minimal. High nitrogen causes floppy growth.
