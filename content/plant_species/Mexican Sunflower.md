# Mexican Sunflower

**Scientific Name:** *Tithonia rotundifolia*
**Type:** Annual
**Seed Mix:** [[Wildflower Seeds|Monarch Rescue Mix]]
**Native to Colorado:** No

**Flower Color:** Orange-Red
**Bloom Time:** Mid-Summer to Fall

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun.
- **Soil:** Average, well-drained soil. Avoid overly rich soil.
- **Water:** Low to moderate. Highly heat and drought tolerant.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Deadhead spent blooms to prolong flowering. Attracts many pollinators.
