# Chives

**Scientific Name:** *Allium schoenoprasum*
**Type:** Perennial Herb
**Native to Colorado:** Yes

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun.
- **Soil:** Rich, well-drained soil.
- **Water:** Moderate.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Cut back to the ground after flowering to rejuvenate foliage.
- **Fertilizing:** Minimal.
