# Siberian Crab Apple

**Scientific Name:** *Malus baccata*
**Type:** Tree
**Native to Colorado:** No

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun.
- **Soil:** Adaptable, well-drained loam.
- **Water:** Moderate. Very cold hardy and moderately drought tolerant.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Prune in late winter while dormant to remove suckers, crossing branches, and water sprouts.
- **Fertilizing:** Apply tree fertilizer in early spring before bud break.
