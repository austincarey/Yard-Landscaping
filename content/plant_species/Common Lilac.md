# Common Lilac

**Scientific Name:** *Syringa vulgaris*
**Type:** Shrub
**Native to Colorado:** No

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun (6+ hours) is required for good blooming.
- **Soil:** Well-drained, slightly alkaline soil. Perfect for Colorado.
- **Water:** Moderate. Water deeply during extended dry periods.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Prune *immediately* after spring flowering. Remove dead wood and up to 1/3 of older stems down to the base to encourage fresh growth.
- **Fertilizing:** Apply a balanced fertilizer in early spring.
