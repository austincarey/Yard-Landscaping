# Tall Fescue

**Scientific Name:** *Festuca arundinacea*
**Type:** Grass
**Native to Colorado:** No

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun to partial shade.
- **Soil:** Adaptable, including heavy clay.
- **Water:** Moderate. Requires 1-1.5 inches of water per week during peak summer heat.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Mow at 3 inches high to encourage deep root growth.
- **Fertilizing:** Fertilize in early spring, late spring, and heavily in autumn (September/October).
