# Decorative Grass

**Scientific Name:** *Various (e.g. Calamagrostis, Miscanthus)*
**Type:** Ornamental Grass
**Native to Colorado:** Varies

## Detailed Care Guide
- **Light:** Full sun to partial shade (depending on variety).
- **Soil:** Well-drained soil.
- **Water:** Low to Moderate. Benefits from deep, infrequent soaking to push roots downward.
- **Pruning/Maintenance:** Cut back to a few inches above ground in late winter or early spring before new growth begins.
- **Other Info:** Setup with one 1 GPH emitter.
